K-Message Sales

Tell customers when their order moves, and let them ask where it stands.

This installs itself beside K-Message Connector when you have the matching Odoo app, and does nothing on its own. Set the connection up once, there, and this adds what belongs to this app.

What Odoo says

WhenWhat goes out
A quotation is sentThe quotation, with its PDF. Arrives switched off.
A sales order is confirmedOrder number and amount. Arrives switched off.

Each rule comes written for you — trigger, template and a field for every placeholder — and each has its own switch.

What a customer may ask

CapabilityAnswers
ordersThat customer's own orders.
order_statusWhere one particular order stands.

Each is an individual switch, and each answers only for the customer whose phone number is in the conversation. A draft quotation is not read out to anybody — Odoo's own portal waits until it has been sent, and so does this.

Requirements

Odoo 17.0 or 18.0, the K-Message Connector app, and a K-Message account with a WhatsApp number.
