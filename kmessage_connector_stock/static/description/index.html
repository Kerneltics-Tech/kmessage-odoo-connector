K-Message Connector — Inventory

Tell a customer their delivery has left, and answer "do you have it?"

This installs itself beside K-Message Connector when you have the matching Odoo app, and does nothing on its own. Set the connection up once, there, and this adds what belongs to this app.

What Odoo says

WhenWhat goes out
A delivery is validatedThe delivery slip — not the reception report Odoo offers first. Arrives switched off.

Each rule comes written for you — trigger, template and a field for every placeholder — and each has its own switch.

What a customer may ask

CapabilityAnswers
stock_checkWhether a product is on hand, from real quantities.
delivery_statusWhere that customer's own delivery is.

Each is an individual switch, and each answers only for the customer whose phone number is in the conversation. Deliveries are matched to the customer exactly, never to everyone underneath them in the contact hierarchy.

Requirements

Odoo 17.0 or 18.0, the K-Message Connector app, and a K-Message account with a WhatsApp number.
